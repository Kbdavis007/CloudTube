Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026 · Applies to https://ct.devmatrix.top

CloudTube (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a personal YouTube library operated by Brian Keith Davis. This policy explains, in plain terms, what data the app accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, who it is shared with, and how you can remove it. It covers both the app at https://ct.devmatrix.top and any copy of it you run yourself.

The short version. CloudTube keeps your library in your own browser. Your saved videos, channels, notes, ratings, and removal history are stored on your device and are not uploaded to us. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use your Google user data for advertising or to train generalized AI models.

2. Data we access through Google

When you choose “Sign in with Google”, you grant CloudTube access to the following Google OAuth scopes. The app requests the YouTube scopes because collecting and organising a YouTube library is the app's only purpose.

Google account identity

  • userinfo.profile — your name and profile picture, used to show who is signed in.
  • userinfo.email — your email address, used to identify your account in the app.

YouTube data

  • youtube.readonly — read your channel, your uploads, video metadata, and comments.
  • youtube and youtube.force-ssl — the scopes required by Google to call the YouTube Data API for channel and video lookups and to read comments. CloudTube does not post, edit, or delete anything on your YouTube account.

The specific data read through these scopes is: your channel identity and subscriber counts, video titles, descriptions, tags, durations, view and like counts, thumbnails, publish dates, and public comments on videos you collect.

3. How we use Google user data

Google user data is used only to provide the app's single user-facing feature: building, displaying, searching, and organising your personal YouTube library. Specifically:

  • to show your saved videos, channels, and comments;
  • to import the videos of a channel you ask to collect;
  • to store your own notes, ratings, and tags against those videos;
  • to generate optional AI summaries of a video you have collected.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, or lending decisions. We do not sell it, rent it, or transfer it to data brokers.

4. Limited Use disclosure

CloudTube's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide or improve the app's user-facing features that are prominent in the app's interface.
  • Google user data is not sold, and is not transferred to third parties for advertising.
  • Google user data is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes, and is not used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • No humans read your Google user data, except where you ask us to act on it, where it is needed for security purposes (such as abuse investigation), to comply with applicable law, or with your explicit consent.

5. Where your data is stored

Your library is stored locally in your browser, on your own device. It uses IndexedDB for videos, channels, comments, crawl jobs, and removal records, and localStorage for small preferences such as your theme, the last page you visited, and which search sources are enabled. CloudTube has no database of its own that holds your library, and there is no CloudTube account to which your library is uploaded.

The practical consequences are: clearing your browser storage removes your library; using a different browser or device gives you a separate library; and you can export or restore the whole library as a JSON file from Settings → Backup.

Your Google OAuth access token is held in memory only, for the duration of the browser session, and is never written to localStorage, sessionStorage, or a server we control.

The one piece of server-side storage

When you run a search, the request goes through a caching route so that the same term is not fetched from the source over and over. That route keeps a short-lived copy of the search results themselves — public video metadata such as titles, thumbnails, and URLs — keyed by the search term and the source, in a Cloudflare database. Each entry expires within a few hours and is refreshed on the next search. This cache holds no Google user data and nothing that identifies you or your library, and it is shared by everyone using the app.

6. Third parties that process data

CloudTube uses a small number of service providers. Each one only sees what is needed to perform its function:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication and YouTube Data API) — handles sign-in and answers the YouTube requests described above. Google's handling of that data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google (Firebase AI Logic / Gemini) — when you ask for an AI summary, the text of that video's metadata (such as its title, description, and tags) and its collected comments are sent to Google's AI service to generate the summary. No summary is generated unless you ask for one.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the app, serves the web pages you load, and stores the short-lived shared search cache described in section 5.

CloudTube contains no advertising networks, no analytics trackers, and no third-party tracking pixels. There is no cookie used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

7. Optional sources and keys you provide

CloudTube can search additional video sources. Some of these are optional and off unless you turn them on, and some require an API key that you supply:

  • If you add a RapidAPI key for the optional adult-content sources, that key is stored in your browser's localStorage and is sent only to the RapidAPI endpoint you are querying, directly from your browser. It is not sent to us.
  • If you connect a Cloudflare account, the API token you provide is encrypted in your browser with AES-GCM before it is written to localStorage. Some Cloudflare API calls are relayed through the app's own proxy route because Cloudflare blocks direct browser requests; the token is passed through that route in transit and is not stored on the server.

8. Legal bases and your rights

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, we process Google user data on the basis of your consent (which you give by signing in and granting the scopes) and our legitimate interest in operating the app you asked to use. You can withdraw consent at any time by signing out and revoking access, as described below.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because your library lives in your own browser, you can exercise most of these rights directly without contacting us. California residents have equivalent rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know and the right to delete; we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by that law.

9. Retention and deletion

We retain no copy of your library, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. You are in direct control of the data on your device:

  • Delete individual items — remove a video from your library at any time, and clear the removal record when you no longer want it influencing your results.
  • Clear everything — use Settings → Clear Library Data to empty the library, then clear this site's storage in your browser settings to remove preferences and any locally stored keys.
  • Revoke Google access — sign out of CloudTube, then remove CloudTube's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all future API calls immediately.

If you would like us to confirm what (if anything) we hold for you, email kbdavis0007@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Security

All traffic to the app is served over HTTPS. Optional credentials you store in the app are encrypted with AES-GCM before being written to your browser's storage, and your Google OAuth token is kept in memory rather than persisted. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security; you should keep your device and browser up to date.

11. Children

CloudTube is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help you remove the data.

12. International transfers

The service providers listed above may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Those transfers rely on the providers' own safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses.
